# Change Management Policy

> Synthetic policy document for the AuditAlly demo. Fictional company "Contoso". For demonstration only.

**Policy ID:** POL-CM-001 · **Version:** 2.0 · **Owner:** Engineering · **Review cadence:** Annual

## 1. Purpose
Ensures changes to infrastructure and applications are controlled, reviewed, and reversible.

## 2. Change Process
- Changes are tracked as tickets with a description, risk rating, and rollback plan.
- Production changes require **peer review and documented approval** before deployment.
- Emergency changes follow an expedited path with retrospective approval within 48 hours.

## 3. Evidence
- A representative sample of approved change tickets is retained each quarter.

## 4. Mapping to Controls
- SOC 2 **CC8.1** (change management)
- ISO/IEC 27001 **A.8.32** (change management)
