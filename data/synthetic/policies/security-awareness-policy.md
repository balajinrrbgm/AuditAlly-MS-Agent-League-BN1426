# Security Awareness Policy

> Synthetic policy document for the AuditAlly demo. Fictional company "Contoso". For demonstration only.

**Policy ID:** POL-SA-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** People & Culture · **Review cadence:** Annual

## 1. Purpose
Ensures personnel understand their security responsibilities.

## 2. Training Requirements
- All personnel complete security awareness training **on hire and at least annually**.
- Phishing simulations run quarterly; repeat clickers receive targeted coaching.
- Completion is tracked in Microsoft Viva Learning and retained as evidence.

## 3. Mapping to Controls
- ISO/IEC 27001 **A.6.3** (awareness, education, and training)
- SOC 2 **CC1.4** (commitment to competence)
