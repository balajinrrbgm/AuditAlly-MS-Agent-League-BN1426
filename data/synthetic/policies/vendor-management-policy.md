# Vendor & Cloud Management Policy

> Synthetic policy document for the AuditAlly demo. Fictional company "Contoso". For demonstration only.

**Policy ID:** POL-VEN-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Cloud Platform · **Review cadence:** Annual

## 1. Purpose
Establishes security requirements for acquiring, operating, and exiting third-party and cloud services.

## 2. Vendor Assessment
- **Critical vendors** complete a security assessment before onboarding and annually thereafter.
- Assessments cover data handling, certifications (SOC 2 / ISO 27001), and breach history.

## 3. Cloud Configuration Baseline
- Cloud environments are measured against a CIS-aligned baseline using Microsoft Defender for Cloud.
- Baseline assessments are refreshed at least quarterly.

## 4. Exit & Portability
- Contracts include data-return and secure-deletion clauses for service exit.

## 5. Mapping to Controls
- ISO/IEC 27001 **A.5.23** (cloud services security), **A.5.19 – A.5.22** (supplier relationships)
- SOC 2 **CC9.2** (vendor risk management)
